Privacy policy · Last updated November 2026

No data collected.

Klangbild has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Klangbild is a remote control and now-playing display for Roon that runs on Apple TV, iPad and iPhone. This policy explains what happens with data when you use the app and this website.

Data collection

Klangbild does not collect, store or share any personal data. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

Local network

Klangbild talks only to the Roon Core on your local network, to show what is playing and to control playback. This data stays on your network and is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

Data on your device

Your settings (appearance, top bar, selected zone) and the authorization token issued by your Roon Core are stored only on your Apple TV, iPad or iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

Crash reports and feedback

If you take part in the TestFlight beta, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, according to Apple's TestFlight terms. This information is only used to fix problems.

Emails and photos you send

If you write to support@klangbild.app or hello@klangbild.app, your message is only used to answer you. If you send a photo of your setup, it is only published (on this website or on Instagram) with your permission, without your name unless you ask for it to be mentioned. You can ask for it to be removed at any time.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; both services may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@klangbild.app

Klangbild is developed by Piero Ballistreri, Switzerland.
